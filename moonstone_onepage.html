About Moonstone Gatherings

Rooted in the details.

Moonstone Gatherings began with a simple belief: the table is the heart of any gathering, and it deserves the same thought as the guest list.

the designer at work

I design picnics and tablescapes the way a florist arranges a bouquet — starting with what's in season, what feels true to you, and letting the rest follow. Every gathering I build starts with your vision, not a template pulled from a catalog.
